Industry Experience

Before entering academia, I worked in fashion and beauty merchandising across leading retail companies in South Korea. These experiences continue to shape how I approach fashion merchandising research and teaching, particularly at the intersection of retail practice, consumer behavior, digital technologies, and data-driven decision-making.

CJ Olive Young

2020–2022
Merchandising (MD) Specialist

I worked as a multi-channel merchandiser for the color cosmetics category across more than 1,300 brick-and-mortar stores and the company's online retail channel. My responsibilities included product assortment, negotiating monthly multi-brand promotions, category planograms and visual merchandising, and sales-performance analysis. I also managed product assortment and communication strategy for Olive Young's Vegan Beauty campaign in 2022.

Sephora Korea

2019–2020
Merchandising Assistant (Category Management Team)

I joined Sephora Korea during the retailer's early expansion into the South Korean market. I supported product allocation for new store openings, coordinated with Sephora Southeast Asia's regional headquarters in Singapore, and communicated with global beauty brands regarding exclusive product-launch timelines. In addition to merchandising, I also developed data-management guidelines for more than 9,000 SKUs to improve the accuracy and consistency of sales analysis for the entire team.

Samsung C&T Fashion Group

2018–2019
Buying Assistant

I supported seasonal buying and merchandising activities for global menswear brand Suitsupply, including product assortment selection and product allocation. I also assisted with the brand's South Korean market development by providing Korean-English interpretation for employee training, press interviews, and flagship-store launch events.
