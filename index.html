I am a PhD candidate in Textile and Apparel Management at the University of Missouri. My research examines how AI shapes decisions and experiences across the fashion supply chain, and how it can support human judgment, creativity, and individuality. In my dissertation, I study AI-driven hyper-personalization in fashion marketing through a mixed-methods approach of computational analysis and an experimental consumer survey. I also use natural language processing to study fashion-related text and examine how LLMs respond to the needs of the fashion industry.

Overall, my goal is to contribute to building the foundation for responsible AI systems for fashion consumers, professionals, and students. I pursue this goal through two complementary research streams: empirical research that examines how users (e.g., consumers, professionals) respond to AI-mediated experiences, and computational research that investigates the technical properties and limitations of AI systems in fashion-specific contexts.
