Teaching

My past experience as a graduate instructor and graduate teaching assistant have led me to discover a genuine joy in teaching. I view teaching as the process of nurturing each student’s individuality, by supporting their learning style and ideas. I aim to guide students through digital and analytical tools to make their own ideas actionable, while preserving their ownership of the decisions they make. Across online and in-person courses, I have helped students use data and emerging technologies while developing their own judgment and creative voice.

Teaching Philosophy

As an educator, I value student individuality, in both learning style and ideas. I teach students to use digital and analytical tools to support their own questions and decisions.

My goal is to prepare students to use emerging technologies critically rather than passively, combining technical skills with their own creative judgment to make real-world decisions.

Individuality

My goal is to facilitate learning experiences that nurture student individuality, in both learning style and ideas, across hybrid, online, and in-person environments. I provide resources that accommodate different levels of digital literacy and different learning environments, empowering students to use tools fluidly while preserving their own creativity.

Critical and Independent Thinking

I value preparing students to become creative yet critical thinkers that are capable of real-world decision-making. I aim to bridge fashion education with emerging analytical approaches, empowering students to take ownership of their creative ideas as future professionals.

Collaborative Mindset

By approaching retail and merchandising as a part of the broader supply chain, I aim to facilitate a collaborative mindset that prepares students to navigate the interconnected fashion supply chain.
