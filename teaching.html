Teaching

My teaching goal is to equip students to become critical yet compassionate thinkers who can engage confidently with data and emerging technologies while developing their own creativity and professional judgment.

Teaching Philosophy

I believe students learn most meaningfully when they can use digital tools and data to investigate questions that matter to them. My role as an instructor is therefore not simply to teach software or analytical techniques, but to create an environment in which students can develop the confidence to apply those tools independently.

Drawing from my experience in both fashion industry practice and academia, I emphasize the relationship between technology, critical judgment, and real-world decision-making. As technologies evolve, I want students to understand not only how to use a tool, but also when it is useful, where it may fail, and how their own individuality remains essential.

Individuality

My goal is to facilitate learning experiences that nurture student individuality. I provide resources that accommodate different levels of digital literacy and different learning environments, helping students use tools fluidly while preserving their own creativity.

Critical and Independent Thinking

I encourage students to question technological outputs rather than treating digital tools as substitutes for professional judgment, connecting their evaluations to real-world fashion merchandising decisions.

Collaborative Mindset

By approaching fashion merchandising, omni-channel retail, and retail data analytics as parts of a broader supply chain, I facilitate a collaborative mindset that prepares students for the operational reality of the fashion industry.
