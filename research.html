Research

Through mixed-method approaches, my research examines how emerging technologies shape user responses and decision-making in technology-mediated fashion experiences. By combining empirical consumer research with computational approaches, my research agenda focuses on the technical properties of AI systems and connects those properties to meaningful downstream outcomes for fashion consumers and professionals.

Technology-Mediated Fashion Experiences AI Hyper-Personalization Consumer Decision-Making Fashion Data Analytics Computational Social Science Natural Language Processing Large Language Models

Research Program

Human-technology interaction

My core research stream focuses on user responses to AI-mediated fashion experiences (e.g., consumers, professionals). Ongoing projects focus on AI-driven hyper-personalization, consumer decision-making, generative AI in digital commerce, and AI implementation across the fashion supply chain.

Fashion Data & Computational Methods

My complementary research stream uses computational methods, involving natural language processing, large language models, sentiment analysis, and topic modeling to analyze fashion-specific data. I am particularly interested in identifying the limitations of general-purpose AI systems in fashion contexts. I aim to contribute to the foundation for responsible AI systems that are truly aligned with the fashion supply chain's needs.
