Research

My research examines how artificial intelligence and data-driven technologies shape fashion consumers, professionals, and retail decision-making. I combine consumer research with computational methods to study emerging AI-mediated fashion experiences and to investigate how computational tools can better address fashion-specific contexts.

Research Program

AI-Mediated Fashion Experiences

My core research examines how consumers and fashion professionals respond to AI-mediated experiences. Current projects investigate generative AI, hyper-personalized fashion marketing, AI-generated imagery, and the implementation of AI across the fashion supply chain.

Fashion Data & Computational Methods

A complementary stream of my research uses natural language processing, large language models, and other computational approaches to analyze fashion-specific data. I am particularly interested in understanding where general-purpose computational tools succeed or fail when applied to fashion consumer contexts.
